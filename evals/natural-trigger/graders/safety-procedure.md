The dangerous part of a jump-start is the connection of the cables: sparks
can make the battery explode, and acid can cause injury.

PASS if all of these are true:
- the reply gives the procedure as numbered steps (more than one list is
  allowed), and most steps are commands;
- at least one WARNING or CAUTION comes before the first step that attaches a
  cable or a clamp (it can be at the start of that section or between steps);
- at least one of these WARNING or CAUTION texts tells the reader what to do
  or not do, and gives the risk.

FAIL if the reply has no WARNING or CAUTION before the cable connection steps
(for example, only a list of safety tips at the end), or if it has no numbered
steps.
